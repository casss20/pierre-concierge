# Recovering at Home vs. a Rehab Facility

After a hospital stay in Ocala you may be offered rehab at a facility or a return home. Neither is always right. Here is how to think it through.

## A rehab facility may be best when

-   You need daily therapy several times a day
-   You cannot safely get around your home yet
-   No one is available to help at home
-   Your doctor or therapist recommends it

## Home may be better when

-   You are medically stable and can move with some help
-   Family can be around for part of the day
-   You recover best in your own surroundings
-   Your home can be made safe with a few changes

## What makes home recovery safer

A nurse visit in the first 72 hours can check incisions, review medications against the discharge list, spot warning signs, and set up the home. It is the most common point where recoveries go wrong, and it is where a nurse helps most.

## Questions, answered

### Who decides between home and rehab?

Your doctor, the hospital case manager, and you. Ask for their recommendation and the reasons behind it. A nurse can help you prepare questions.

This page is general information, not medical advice. Always follow the instructions of your own doctor. Services are non-emergency. In a medical emergency, call 911.
