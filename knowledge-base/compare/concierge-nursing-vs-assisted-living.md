# Concierge Nursing vs. Assisted Living

Assisted living is a place to live with help built in. Concierge nursing is a nurse who comes to you, wherever you live.

## Side by side

|  | Assisted living | Concierge nursing |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Where | A community you move into | Your own home or your facility room |
| What you get | Housing, meals, and help with daily tasks | Visits from a Registered Nurse |
| Nursing | Varies by facility, often limited | A Registered Nurse for assessment and orders |
| Cost | Monthly fees | Per visit or a monthly plan |
| Moving required | Yes | No |

## They can work together

If your loved one already lives in an assisted living facility, a private nurse can add one on one attention, check medications, and attend doctor visits. Ask the facility about its policy on outside nurses first.

## Questions to ask a facility

-   What nursing is on site and during which hours?
-   What happens if care needs increase?
-   What does the monthly fee include?
-   May we bring in a private nurse?

## Questions, answered

### Do you serve people in assisted living facilities?

Yes, with the facility's permission. Call to confirm travel to the address.
