# Concierge Nursing vs. Private Duty Nursing

The terms overlap. Here is how we use them, so you can compare like with like when you call around.

## Private duty nursing

Usually long shifts of one on one nursing, often through an agency, for people who need continuous skilled care at home.

## Concierge nursing

Scheduled visits from a Registered Nurse you know by name, with time to assess, explain, and coordinate with your doctors. Pricing is posted, and you choose the visits you need.

## Side by side

|  | Private duty nursing | Concierge nursing |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Format | Long shifts | Scheduled visits |
| Best for | Continuous skilled care | Check ins, treatments, and guidance |
| Same nurse each time | Depends on the agency | Yes |
| Pricing | Hourly, often by agency quote | Posted per visit or monthly plan |

## Questions, answered

### Do you offer 24 hour nursing?

No. Pierre Concierge Nursing provides scheduled visits, Monday to Saturday. If you need round the clock nursing, ask your doctor about private duty agencies.
