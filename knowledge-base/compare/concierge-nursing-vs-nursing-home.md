# Concierge Nursing vs. a Nursing Home

A nursing home provides round the clock skilled care in a facility. Some people need that. Others do better at home with the right support.

## Side by side

|  | Nursing home | Concierge nursing |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Where | A licensed facility | Your own home |
| Care available | Skilled nursing around the clock | Scheduled visits from a Registered Nurse |
| Best for | People who need constant skilled care | People who are stable and want to stay home |
| Familiar surroundings | No | Yes |
| Who pays | Private pay, insurance, or Medicaid, depending on the stay | Private pay |

## Staying home safely

Many people can stay at home with family help, a caregiver for daily tasks, and regular nurse visits for medications and health checks. If needs grow beyond what visits can safely cover, a nurse will tell you honestly.

## When a facility may be the safer choice

-   Care is needed all day and night by skilled staff
-   Falls or confusion make being alone unsafe
-   Family cannot provide the help in between visits

## Questions, answered

### Does Medicare pay for a nursing home?

Medicare covers a limited stay in a skilled nursing facility in certain cases, such as after a qualifying hospital stay. It does not pay for long term custodial care. Call 1-800-MEDICARE to check your situation.

This page is general information, not medical advice. Always follow the instructions of your own doctor. Services are non-emergency. In a medical emergency, call 911.
