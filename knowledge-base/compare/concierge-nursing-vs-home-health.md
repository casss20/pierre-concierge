# Concierge Nursing vs. Medicare Home Health

Medicare home health and concierge nursing solve different problems. This page lays out the differences honestly, including where Medicare is the better choice.

## Where Medicare home health is the right call

If you qualify and need skilled care for a limited time after surgery or a hospital stay, Medicare home health can cost you very little and is a good first step. Ask your doctor or hospital case manager to arrange it.

## Where concierge nursing helps

-   You are not homebound, so you do not qualify for Medicare home health.
-   Home health visits are short and you want more time with a nurse.
-   Your home health episode ended but you still need monitoring and support.
-   You want the same nurse every time instead of a rotating schedule.
-   You want visits at times that work for you and your family, Monday through Saturday.

## Side by side

|  | Concierge nursing | Medicare home health |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Payment | Private pay, posted prices | Medicare or insurance if you qualify |
| Homebound required | No | Generally yes |
| Same nurse each visit | Yes | Depends on the agency |
| Time per visit | Set by you and your nurse | Short, task focused |
| Duration | No time limit set by insurance | Limited by the plan of care |
| Treatments and medications | Follow your doctor's orders | Follow your doctor's orders |

## Questions, answered

### Do you bill Medicare or insurance?

Not at this time. Pierre Concierge Nursing is private pay and does not bill insurance directly.

### Do I need my doctor's order?

For medications, IV therapy, injections, and wound treatments, yes. Assessments and check ins do not need one. Elda confirms this with you before the first visit.
