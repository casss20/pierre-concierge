# Care After a Hospital Stay in Ocala

The first days at home are the riskiest. A nurse visit helps you get through them safely.

## Ocala hospitals we often work around

Families in Marion County are commonly discharged from AdventHealth Ocala, HCA Florida Ocala Hospital, or HCA Florida West Marion Hospital. Pierre Concierge Nursing is not affiliated with any hospital. We work from your discharge papers and your doctor's orders.

## What we check

-   Medications on the discharge list against what is in the house
-   Incisions, dressings, and drains
-   Pain, swelling, breathing, and other warning signs
-   Equipment, fall risks, and daily needs
-   Follow up appointments and who to call with questions

## Questions, answered

### Should I start with home health?

If you qualify, Medicare home health can be a good first step. Ask your discharge team. We can add visits when you need more time or more support.

This page is general information, not medical advice. Always follow the instructions of your own doctor. Services are non-emergency. In a medical emergency, call 911.
