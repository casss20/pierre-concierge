# Nursing for Visitors and Travelers in Ocala

Getting sick or needing care while away from home is stressful. A visiting nurse can come to you. Please call first so Elda can confirm she can help.

## When this helps

-   Injections or medications your doctor has prescribed while you travel
-   A check on someone who is recovering and cannot travel yet
-   Help for a family member staying in a rental or vacation home

## What to have ready

-   A current medication list
-   Your doctor's orders if a treatment is needed
-   The address where you are staying

## Questions, answered

### Is this an emergency service?

No. For an emergency, call 911. For urgent illness, use an urgent care or emergency room.

This page is general information, not medical advice. Always follow the instructions of your own doctor. Services are non-emergency. In a medical emergency, call 911.
