The Safe Home Playbook

## The nine problems it solves

We built it from what families report most, in published research and in caregiver communities.

-   A rushed discharge with no written plan
-   Confusing medicines
-   Pain that is not controlled at home
-   Tasks nobody trained you for, like dressings and safe moves
-   Not knowing who to call, and nobody calling back
-   A loved one who pushes limits or refuses help
-   Caregiver overload and no backup plan
-   Not knowing whether home is still the right place
-   Keeping the whole family in sync

## How every problem is handled

1.  1Spot it: what it looks like and why it happens.
2.  2Do the steps: numbered, in order, ready to start today.
3.  3Say it: the exact words to use with nurses, doctors, pharmacists, and family.
4.  4Escalate: who to call, and when to call 911.

## Who it is for

-   Families bringing a parent, spouse, or relative home after surgery or a hospital stay
-   Adult children organizing care from a distance
-   Anyone who wants a clear plan before discharge day
