## Questions, answered

### Does it only work for Florida?

The tools work anywhere. Legal forms and phone numbers are Florida specific, so check your own state if your parent lives elsewhere.

### Do I need a nurse to use it?

No. A nurse can help, but the kit is built for families to use on their own.
