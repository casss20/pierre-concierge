# Resources for Ocala and Marion County Families

Helpful numbers and official sites. We are not affiliated with any of them.

## Emergencies and crisis

-   Medical emergency: 911
-   Suicide and Crisis Lifeline: call or text 988
-   Poison Control: 1-800-222-1222

## Medicare and health coverage

-   Medicare: 1-800-MEDICARE or Medicare.gov
-   Florida facility and provider lookup: FloridaHealthFinder.gov

## Older adults and caregivers

-   Florida Elder Helpline: 1-800-963-5337
-   Alzheimer's Association 24 hour helpline: 1-800-272-3900

## Veterans

-   Marion County Veterans Services: (352) 671-8422
-   Ocala VA Clinic: 4826 SW 49th Road, Ocala
-   Veterans Crisis Line: dial 988 then press 1
