# How Pierre Concierge Nursing Works

A Registered Nurse is part of your care from the first conversation. There is no clinic visit and no waiting room. Elda comes to you.

## From first call to care at home

1.  1You reach out. Call or text (754) 367-6929, or send a request. A family member, the patient, or a discharge planner can contact us.
2.  2Elda talks with you. She asks what is going on, what you need, and whether a doctor's order is required for the care you want.
3.  3She visits you at home, Monday through Saturday, at a time that works for you.
4.  4You get clear notes on what was done and what to watch for, so family stays in the loop.
5.  5You have the same nurse each time. Elda stays familiar with your history and notices changes early.
6.  6Care adapts as things change. Visits can be one time, per visit as needed, or on the monthly Care Plan, and Elda can coordinate with your doctors.

## Before the first visit

-   Have a current list of medications
-   Have your doctor's orders or discharge papers handy
-   Know who the family contact is

## Questions, answered

### How do I get started?

Call or text (754) 367-6929, or use the request form. Tell us what is going on and Elda will call you back.

### How quickly can care begin?

It depends on Elda's schedule and your needs. Call or text and we will tell you the soonest time she can visit. Visits are Monday to Saturday.

### Do I need a doctor's referral?

Not to start. Assessments and check ins do not need one. Medications, IV therapy, injections, and wound treatments need your doctor's order, and Elda will confirm that with you.

### Will I see the same nurse?

Yes. Elda is your nurse.

### Does insurance pay?

We are private pay and do not bill insurance at this time. We can give you a receipt for your records.

### Can the plan change?

Yes. Visits and plans are adjusted as your needs change. Just tell Elda.

This page is general information, not medical advice. Always follow the instructions of your own doctor. Services are non-emergency. In a medical emergency, call 911.
