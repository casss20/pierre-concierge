# Discharge Readiness Kit

Made for the family member who is organizing everything. Print it, take it to the hospital, and fill it in with the care team.

## What is in the kit

-   Questions to ask before discharge
-   A medication list you can fill in
-   A home setup checklist
-   A daily recovery log for the first two weeks
-   When to call the doctor and when to call 911
-   A contacts page for doctors, pharmacy, and family

[Open the printable kit](https://pierre-concierge.vercel.app/discharge-readiness-kit.html)

This kit is general information, not medical advice. Always follow your own doctor's and surgical team's instructions. In an emergency, call 911.
