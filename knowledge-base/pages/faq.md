# Frequently asked questions

Insurance, payment, physician orders, and how a visit works.

### Do you accept insurance?

Not at this time. We do not bill insurance directly. We are a private-pay (concierge) service, which allows us to provide unhurried, higher-quality care without insurance restrictions. We provide a detailed superbill you can submit to your insurance for potential out-of-network reimbursement, though coverage depends on your plan.

### What forms of payment do you accept?

All major credit cards, HSA/FSA cards, and Zelle. Payment is due at booking to secure your appointment.

### How is concierge nursing different from Medicare home health?

Medicare home health is short, task-focused, physician-ordered, and generally requires that you are homebound. Pierre Concierge Nursing is private-pay: the same nurse each time, flexible visit times, no homebound requirement, and no insurance-imposed time limit. The two can work side by side, and we are glad to coordinate with your home health team.

### Is my health information kept private?

Yes. Your records and visit updates are shared only with the doctors and family members you authorize.

### Can you prescribe medication?

As a Registered Nurse, I administer medications prescribed by your physician. I cannot write new prescriptions, but I work closely with your doctor to make sure you get what you need.

### Which areas do you serve?

Mobile nursing throughout Ocala, Silver Springs Shores, Belleview, and The Villages (North) — private residences, hotels, and assisted living facilities.

### Do I need a referral from a hospital or doctor?

No referral is needed to book. Most families contact us directly, often the week of a hospital discharge or when a loved one needs more care than an assisted living facility can provide. Treatments and medications, such as IV therapy, injections, and wound dressings, do require your doctor's order, which we verify at intake. We're also happy to receive referrals from physicians, surgeons, senior living communities, and home-health agencies.
