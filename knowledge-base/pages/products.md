## Questions, answered

### Which one should I start with?

Start with the Discharge Readiness Kit if discharge is near and you want lists to fill in. Choose the Safe Home Playbook for the steps, scripts, and emergency guide for the first 14 days. Many families use both.

### How will I receive them?

They will be downloadable PDFs that you can print. Details will be posted here when they launch.

### Is this medical advice?

No. These are general guides. Always follow your own doctor and care team, and call 911 in an emergency.
