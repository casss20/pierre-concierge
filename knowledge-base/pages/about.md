## Questions, answered

### What is concierge nursing?

A Registered Nurse comes to your home for scheduled, private pay visits. You get more time, one familiar nurse, and clear communication, without a clinic trip.

### How is it different from home health?

Home health is short term skilled care ordered by your doctor and often covered by Medicare if you qualify. Concierge nursing is private pay, has no homebound rule, and is scheduled around your life. Many families use both.

### Who provides the care?

Elda Marie Pierre, RN, BSN, provides your care herself.

### Do I need a doctor's referral?

Not to start. Assessments and check ins do not need one. Medications, IV therapy, injections, and wound treatments need your doctor's order, and Elda will confirm that with you.

### Does insurance pay?

We are private pay and do not bill insurance at this time. Some long term care policies and veterans benefits may reimburse you. We can give you a receipt.
