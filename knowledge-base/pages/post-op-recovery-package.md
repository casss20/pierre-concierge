# Post-Op Recovery Package

Coming home from the hospital is the easy part to underestimate. The Post-Op Recovery Package puts a Registered Nurse in your home through the first weeks, so you and your family are never guessing.

## What the package covers

### A discharge visit

Elda reviews your discharge papers, sets up medications, checks the home for safety, and looks at your incision or dressings.

### Scheduled recovery visits

Check ins on pain, incisions, vitals, mobility, and warning signs, planned around your surgeon's instructions.

### Medication help

Reconciling new prescriptions with what is already in the house, pill organization, and injections your doctor has ordered.

### Wound and dressing care

Sterile dressing changes and measurement when your doctor has ordered them.

### Updates for family

Clear notes after each visit, so children and relatives, even out of town, know what was done and what to watch for.

### Coordination with your doctors

With your permission, Elda shares what she sees so your care team can act on it.

## How it is priced

There is no bundle price to decode. The package is built from our posted rates, and Elda tells you the plan and the total before care begins.

-   Hospital to Home Transition visit: $185
-   Post-Op Check-in: $160 per visit
-   Wound Care Visit: $175 per visit
-   Family Check-In (visit plus same day report): $150
-   The monthly Care Plan: $450 per month, with 3 visits included, no long term contract

Example only: a discharge visit at $185 plus two Post-Op Check-ins at $160 each comes to $505. Your own plan depends on your surgery, your doctor's instructions, and how much help you want.

## Questions, answered

### Is the Post-Op Recovery Package a fixed price?

No. It is built from our posted per visit rates or the $450 monthly Care Plan. Elda gives you the plan and the total before care begins.

### How many visits will I need?

It depends on your surgery, your doctor's instructions, and your comfort at home. Elda recommends a plan after talking with you, and it can change as you recover.

### Can you start before I leave the hospital?

Call or text as soon as you know your discharge date. Elda can talk with you or your family beforehand and tell you the soonest visit she can offer.

### Does insurance or Medicare pay for this?

We are private pay and do not bill insurance at this time. We can give you a receipt for your records. See Paying for care for other options.

### Do I need a doctor's order?

Not for assessments and check ins. Medications, injections, IV therapy, and wound treatments need your doctor's order, and Elda will confirm that with you.

This page is general information, not medical advice. Always follow the instructions of your own doctor. Services are non-emergency. In a medical emergency, call 911.
