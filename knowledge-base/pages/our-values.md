# Our values

Why Pierre Concierge Nursing exists, and the principles behind every visit.

Why we exist

## Care the way it should be

Too many people go home from the hospital with confusing instructions and little support. Visits are rushed, the person changes every time, and no one explains what the doctor said.

Pierre Concierge Nursing exists to close that gap. One Registered Nurse, who takes the time to listen, explain, and follow through, in the comfort of your own home.

What it means

## Skilled, RN-led care, at home

Concierge nursing is private pay, nurse led care that comes to you. You choose the visits you need, and treatments follow your doctor’s orders.

-   Recovery after surgery or a hospital stay
-   Help managing a chronic condition
-   Medication reviews and health checks for an aging parent

Our values

## What we stand for

### Personal attention

You get one nurse who knows you, your history, and what matters to your family.

### Clear communication

Plain language, written notes after each visit, and answers to your questions.

### Advocacy

Elda speaks up for you with your doctors so nothing is missed or misunderstood.

### Privacy and discretion

Your information is shared only with the doctors and family you authorize.

### Honest pricing

Posted per visit rates and a monthly Care Plan, with no surprises.

### Rooted in Marion County

Care at home across Ocala and Marion County, close to the people we serve.
