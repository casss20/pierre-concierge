# Ocala

Ocala is the county seat and home base for Pierre Concierge Nursing. Elda visits homes across the city.

## Care close to home

Ocala has three main hospitals: AdventHealth Ocala, HCA Florida Ocala Hospital, and HCA Florida West Marion Hospital. There is also Encompass Health Rehabilitation Hospital of Ocala for inpatient rehab. When you or a loved one comes home from any of them, a nurse visit can help the transition.

## What we help with

-   Recovery after surgery and hospital stays
-   Medication management and teaching
-   Wound care and IV therapy with a doctor's order
-   Wellness checks for older adults and their families

This page is general information, not medical advice. Always follow the instructions of your own doctor. Services are non-emergency. In a medical emergency, call 911.
