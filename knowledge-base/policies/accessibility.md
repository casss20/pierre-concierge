# Accessibility

We want everyone to be able to use this website.

## What we do

-   Use clear headings, readable text, and good color contrast
-   Provide descriptions for images
-   Support keyboard use
-   Respect your device setting to reduce motion, including the scrolling banner and page videos

## Need help?

This page is general information, not medical advice. Always follow the instructions of your own doctor. Services are non-emergency. In a medical emergency, call 911.
