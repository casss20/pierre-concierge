# Veterans Benefits and Home Nursing

Veterans and surviving spouses may qualify for benefits that help with the cost of care at home. A local officer can tell you what applies.

## Where to start in Marion County

-   Marion County Veterans Services: (352) 671-8422
-   Ocala VA Clinic: 4826 SW 49th Road, Ocala
-   Veterans Crisis Line: dial 988, then press 1

## Aid and Attendance

Aid and Attendance is an added amount some veterans and surviving spouses can receive with a VA pension when they need help with daily activities. Eligibility depends on service, income, assets, and medical need. A county veterans service officer can help you apply.

## How we help

Benefits and rules change. Always confirm details with the VA or your county veterans service officer.
