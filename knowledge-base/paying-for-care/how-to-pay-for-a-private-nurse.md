# How to Pay for a Private Nurse

Here is a plain guide to the ways families cover private nursing, and what to ask.

## Private pay

Pierre Concierge Nursing is private pay with posted prices. We do not bill insurance directly at this time. See the pricing page for per visit rates and the monthly Care Plan.

## Medicare and insurance

Medicare can cover part time skilled home health if you qualify. It does not pay for ongoing help with daily living on its own, and it does not pay for private nursing visits from us. Call 1-800-MEDICARE or visit Medicare.gov to check your coverage.

## Long term care insurance

Policies differ. Ask your carrier what triggers benefits, whether there is a waiting period, and whether they reimburse a private nurse. Keep your receipts.

## HSA and FSA

Some nursing services may be eligible for reimbursement from a health savings or flexible spending account. Check with your plan administrator before you pay.

## Veterans benefits

Marion County Veterans Services helps veterans and families with VA benefits at (352) 671-8422. Benefits and rules change, so confirm details with the VA.
