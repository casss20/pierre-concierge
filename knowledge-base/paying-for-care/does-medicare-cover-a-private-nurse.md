# Does Medicare Cover a Private Nurse?

Short answer: Medicare covers some skilled home health care, but not private nursing visits like ours. Here is the difference.

## What Medicare may cover

-   Part time skilled nursing or therapy at home, if you qualify
-   Your doctor must certify that you need it and set a plan of care
-   You generally must be homebound and use a Medicare certified agency
-   A limited stay in a skilled nursing facility after a qualifying hospital stay

## What Medicare generally does not cover

-   Round the clock care at home
-   Help with bathing, meals, and daily tasks when that is all you need
-   Private nursing visits from an independent nurse

## How private nursing fits

Many families use Medicare home health for the skilled care it covers and add private nursing for extra time, explanations, and coordination. Pierre Concierge Nursing is private pay and does not bill Medicare.

Rules and coverage change. For your own situation, call 1-800-MEDICARE or visit Medicare.gov.

## Questions, answered

### Can I use Medicare home health and a private nurse together?

Yes. They serve different purposes. Tell both teams what each is doing so care stays coordinated.

This page is general information, not medical advice. Always follow the instructions of your own doctor. Services are non-emergency. In a medical emergency, call 911.
