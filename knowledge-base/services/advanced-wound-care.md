# Advanced Wound Care

Wounds heal best when they are cleaned, dressed, and measured the same careful way each time. We bring supplies and sterile technique to your home so you can avoid repeated trips to a wound clinic.

[(754) 367-6929](tel:+17543676929)Starting at $175

Physician's order: Required. Dressings and wound treatments follow the orders of your doctor or wound clinic. We confirm them at intake.

## Who it is for

-   Patients with surgical wounds that need dressing changes
-   People with pressure ulcers or diabetic wounds
-   Family caregivers who are nervous about changing dressings themselves

## What to have ready

-   Your doctor's or wound clinic's orders for the dressing
-   A list of current medications

## What happens during a visit

1.  01Wash hands, set up a clean work area, and use sterile technique
2.  02Remove the old dressing, clean the wound, and apply the dressing your provider ordered
3.  03Measure and record the wound so changes can be tracked over time
4.  04Look for signs of infection, such as spreading redness, increasing pain, warmth, odor, or drainage
5.  05Teach you or your family what to do between visits

Good to know

We follow the wound care orders from your physician. If a wound looks worse, we contact your doctor right away.

This page is general information, not medical advice. Always follow the instructions of your own doctor. Services are non-emergency. In a medical emergency, call 911.
