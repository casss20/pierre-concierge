# Family Peace-of-Mind Visits

When you manage a parent or relative from far away, you want honest answers. A scheduled nurse visit gives you a clear picture, with a report the same day.

[(754) 367-6929](tel:+17543676929)$150 per visit

Physician's order: Not needed. No order needed. These are check in visits with vitals, observations, and a report.

*(Image: A warm living room table with two cups of tea and a phone by a sunny window)*

## Who it is for

-   Adult children and relatives who live in another city or state
-   Families who want a professional set of eyes on a loved one
-   Seniors who are living independently and want a regular check in

## What to have ready

-   The name and phone number of the family contact who receives the report
-   Permission from your loved one to share information with you

## What happens during a visit

1.  01Spend unhurried time with your loved one and check how they are doing
2.  02Measure vitals and review medications and supplies
3.  03Look at the home for safety concerns and signs they may be struggling
4.  04Send you a same day report with plain, honest observations

Good to know

Reports go only to the people your loved one authorizes.

This page is general information, not medical advice. Always follow the instructions of your own doctor. Services are non-emergency. In a medical emergency, call 911.
