# IV Therapy & Hydration

Some people need fluids or medications through a vein but would rather not sit in a clinic or infusion center. With a physician's order, a licensed Registered Nurse can give them at home and monitor you the whole time.

[(754) 367-6929](tel:+17543676929)Starting at $200

Physician's order: Required. We verify your physician's order before every visit.

## Who it is for

-   Patients whose doctor has ordered IV fluids or IV medications
-   People recovering from illness or surgery who are dehydrated
-   Patients who find it hard to travel to a clinic

## What to have ready

-   A written or prescribed order from your physician
-   A list of allergies and current medications

## What happens during a visit

1.  01Confirm the physician's order, your identity, and your allergies before starting
2.  02Check vitals and ask how you are feeling
3.  03Prepare the supplies, start the IV, and give the ordered fluid or medication
4.  04Stay with you and monitor throughout the infusion
5.  05Remove the IV when finished, and record what was given

Good to know

IV therapy at Pierre Concierge Nursing requires a physician's order, and we verify it before every visit. Without an order, we cannot give IV treatment.

This page is general information, not medical advice. Always follow the instructions of your own doctor. Services are non-emergency. In a medical emergency, call 911.
