# Assisted Living Facility Support

Assisted living communities help with daily living, but many cannot perform clinical tasks. We provide those clinical services right inside the facility so a resident may not need to move to a higher level of care.

[(754) 367-6929](tel:+17543676929)$175 per visit

Physician's order: Required. Clinical tasks such as insulin and wound care require a physician's order, which we confirm at intake.

*(Image: A bright, cozy assisted living room with an armchair, a lamp, and a window)*

## Who it is for

-   Residents who need insulin, injections, or wound care
-   Families who want their loved one to stay in their current community
-   Facilities that want a reliable outside nurse for specific residents

## What to have ready

-   Physician orders for the clinical care needed
-   Contact information for the facility's care team

## What happens during a visit

1.  01Coordinate with facility staff on timing and access
2.  02Provide ordered clinical care such as insulin administration and wound care
3.  03Monitor vitals and watch for changes in condition
4.  04Document the visit and update the resident's physician and family as authorized

Good to know

Monthly contracts are available. Facilities and families can ask about a schedule that fits the resident's needs.

This page is general information, not medical advice. Always follow the instructions of your own doctor. Services are non-emergency. In a medical emergency, call 911.
