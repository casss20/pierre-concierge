# Wellness Assessments

A wellness assessment is a full nursing check up at home. It gives you a baseline, and a nurse's explanation of what the numbers and findings mean.

[(754) 367-6929](tel:+17543676929)$150 per visit

Physician's order: Not needed. No order needed for an assessment.

*(Image: A stethoscope, a pulse oximeter, and a blood pressure cuff arranged on linen)*

## Who it is for

-   Anyone who wants a thorough check without going to a clinic
-   Families who want a clear update on a loved one's health
-   People who want a baseline before or after a change in health

## What to have ready

-   Your medication list
-   Questions about anything you have noticed lately

## What happens during a visit

1.  01Measure blood pressure, heart rate, oxygen level, and temperature
2.  02Listen to heart and lung sounds and assess breathing
3.  03Check skin, swelling, mobility, and general condition from head to toe
4.  04Review medications and ask about symptoms, sleep, appetite, and mood
5.  05Explain the findings clearly and send updates to family or your physician if you wish

Good to know

An assessment is not a medical diagnosis. If we find something concerning, we recommend contacting your doctor.

This page is general information, not medical advice. Always follow the instructions of your own doctor. Services are non-emergency. In a medical emergency, call 911.
