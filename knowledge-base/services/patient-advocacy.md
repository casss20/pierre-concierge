# Patient Advocacy

Medical visits move fast and the language can be confusing. A nurse at your side helps you understand your options, ask the right questions, and keep your care organized.

[(754) 367-6929](tel:+17543676929)Hourly Rates

Physician's order: Not needed. No order needed. We explain, organize, and support. Your doctors make the medical decisions.

*(Image: A tidy desk with a folder of papers, reading glasses, a pen, and a cup of tea)*

## Who it is for

-   Patients who feel lost after a new diagnosis or surgery
-   Families helping a parent navigate several specialists
-   Anyone who wants support at an important appointment

## What to have ready

-   Your records, test results, or orders you want explained
-   A list of your questions and concerns

## What happens during a visit

1.  01Review your doctor's orders and explain them in plain language
2.  02Attend appointments with you when you want company and a second set of ears
3.  03Help you prepare questions and keep notes
4.  04Organize medical records, medication lists, and appointment dates

Good to know

Advocacy is billed by the hour. We explain and support, and your doctors remain in charge of medical decisions.

This page is general information, not medical advice. Always follow the instructions of your own doctor. Services are non-emergency. In a medical emergency, call 911.
