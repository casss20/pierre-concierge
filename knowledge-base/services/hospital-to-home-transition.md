# Hospital-to-Home Transition

The first days after leaving the hospital are when confusion about medications, equipment, and instructions is most likely. We help you land safely at home so nothing falls through the cracks.

[(754) 367-6929](tel:+17543676929)$185 per visit

Physician's order: Depends on the care. Education, coordination, and home safety checks need no order. Any treatment or medication follows your doctor's orders.

*(Image: A tidy, welcoming home entryway with a folder and tote bag on a console table)*

## Who it is for

-   Patients being discharged after a hospital stay or procedure
-   Families coordinating care from a distance
-   Anyone going home with new medications, a new wound, or new equipment

## What to have ready

-   Discharge paperwork and prescriptions
-   Names and numbers for your doctors and pharmacy

## What happens during a visit

1.  01Go through the discharge instructions and explain them step by step
2.  02Set up medications and compare them to the discharge list
3.  03Check incisions or wounds and look for early signs of trouble
4.  04Review the home for fall risks and help arrange a safe resting space
5.  05Confirm that needed equipment and follow up appointments are in place

Good to know

Our free Going Home After Surgery checklist can help you prepare before you leave the hospital.

This page is general information, not medical advice. Always follow the instructions of your own doctor. Services are non-emergency. In a medical emergency, call 911.
