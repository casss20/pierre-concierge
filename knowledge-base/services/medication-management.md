# Medication Management

When someone takes many prescriptions, a missed or doubled dose is easy and can be dangerous. We help organize medications so they are taken correctly and consistently.

[(754) 367-6929](tel:+17543676929)$150 per visit

Physician's order: Depends on the care. Giving medications and injections such as insulin or B12 requires a physician's order. Reviewing medications and organizing a pill box does not.

*(Image: A weekly pill organizer, a glass of water, and a notebook on a kitchen table)*

## Who it is for

-   People taking several prescriptions, especially after a hospital stay
-   Patients who need regular injections such as insulin or B12
-   Families who worry about missed doses or confusing labels

## What to have ready

-   All medications, including over the counter products and supplements
-   Your doctor's medication list or discharge list

## What happens during a visit

1.  01Compare every bottle in the home to your doctor's current list
2.  02Flag duplicates, expired medications, and anything that does not match
3.  03Set up a weekly pill organizer and a simple schedule
4.  04Give ordered injections and teach safe technique where appropriate
5.  05Coordinate refills with your pharmacy and share questions with your doctor

Good to know

We do not prescribe or change doses. Any concern about a medication goes to your prescribing doctor.

This page is general information, not medical advice. Always follow the instructions of your own doctor. Services are non-emergency. In a medical emergency, call 911.
