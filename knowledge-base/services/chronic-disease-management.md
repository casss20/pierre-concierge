# Chronic Disease Management

Long term conditions such as diabetes, high blood pressure, and heart disease are managed day by day. Regular nurse visits help catch small changes before they turn into an emergency room trip.

[(754) 367-6929](tel:+17543676929)Custom Plans

Physician's order: Depends on the care. Monitoring and teaching are nursing care and need no order. Medications and treatments follow your doctor's plan.

## Who it is for

-   People living with diabetes, hypertension, or heart conditions
-   Families who want regular, reliable check ins between doctor appointments
-   Patients who feel overwhelmed by their medications or instructions

## What to have ready

-   Your current medication list and any home readings or logs
-   Your doctor's instructions and upcoming appointment dates

## What happens during a visit

1.  01Check blood pressure, heart rate, oxygen level, weight, and other readings your doctor wants tracked
2.  02Review blood sugar logs or home monitor readings where they apply
3.  03Go over medications, refills, and how each one is taken
4.  04Talk through diet, fluids, activity, and symptoms in plain language
5.  05Keep a simple record and share it with your doctor or family

Good to know

We support the plan your doctor has set. We do not diagnose conditions or change treatments.

This page is general information, not medical advice. Always follow the instructions of your own doctor. Services are non-emergency. In a medical emergency, call 911.
