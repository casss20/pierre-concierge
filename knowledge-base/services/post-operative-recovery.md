# Post-Operative Recovery

Recovery after surgery is easier when a nurse watches for small problems early. We come to your home in the days and weeks after surgery to check how you are healing and to make sure the plan from your surgeon is actually being followed.

[(754) 367-6929](tel:+17543676929)$160 per visit

Physician's order: Depends on the care. Checking vitals and your incision is nursing assessment and needs no order. Any treatment or medication follows your surgeon's orders.

## Who it is for

-   Patients going home after a planned or unplanned surgery
-   Older adults who live alone or whose family cannot be there every day
-   Anyone who wants a nurse to confirm they are healing as expected

## What to have ready

-   Your discharge paperwork and medication list
-   Your surgeon's name and phone number
-   Any questions you have written down

## What happens during a visit

1.  01Check the incision for redness, swelling, warmth, drainage, or other changes
2.  02Measure vitals such as blood pressure, heart rate, temperature, and oxygen level
3.  03Ask about pain, appetite, sleep, and bowel and bladder function
4.  04Review every medication against your discharge list
5.  05Help with safe movement, getting up, and fall prevention around the home
6.  06Explain what is normal, what to watch for, and when to call your surgeon

Good to know

We do not change your surgeon's orders. If something looks wrong, we contact your doctor and tell you what to do next.

This page is general information, not medical advice. Always follow the instructions of your own doctor. Services are non-emergency. In a medical emergency, call 911.
