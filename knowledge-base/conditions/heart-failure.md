# Heart Failure Care at Home

Small changes in weight, swelling, or breathing can be early signs of trouble. Regular checks help catch them sooner.

## How a nurse can help

-   Track weight, blood pressure, pulse, and swelling
-   Review medications, including water pills
-   Teach low sodium eating and fluid guidance your doctor has set
-   Make a simple plan for what to do and who to call

## Get help quickly if

-   Weight goes up fast, as your doctor has told you to watch for
-   Swelling or breathlessness gets worse
-   You wake up short of breath

If you think you or a loved one is having a medical emergency, call 911 right away. Do not wait for a nurse visit.

## Questions, answered

### Can a nurse replace my cardiologist?

No. A nurse supports your care between appointments and keeps your doctors informed.

This page is general information, not medical advice. Always follow the instructions of your own doctor. Services are non-emergency. In a medical emergency, call 911.
