# Conditions We Support

General information and how a nurse can help. This is not medical advice.

## Diabetes Care at Home

Diabetes is managed day by day. A nurse who knows you can help you spot trends before they become emergencies.

## Heart Failure Care at Home

Small changes in weight, swelling, or breathing can be early signs of trouble. Regular checks help catch them sooner.

COPD can flare up quickly. Knowing your normal and having a plan makes flare ups easier to catch.

Read more](https://pierreconciergenursing.com/conditions/copd)[

High blood pressure often has no symptoms. Regular, accurate readings at home give your doctor better information.

Read more](https://pierreconciergenursing.com/conditions/high-blood-pressure)[

Recovery continues long after the hospital stay. A nurse helps keep the plan on track and families informed.

Dementia affects the whole family. A nurse can keep an eye on health, make medications safer, and help caregivers feel less alone.
