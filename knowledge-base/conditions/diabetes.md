# Diabetes Care at Home

Diabetes is managed day by day. A nurse who knows you can help you spot trends before they become emergencies.

## How a nurse can help

-   Review blood sugar logs and spot patterns
-   Check that medications and insulin are taken as ordered
-   Teach checking blood sugar, injection technique, and foot checks
-   Look for skin and foot problems early
-   Share findings with your doctor

## Call your doctor if

-   Blood sugar stays higher or lower than your doctor's targets
-   You have a sore on your foot that is not healing
-   You are unsure how to adjust for illness or a missed meal

## Questions, answered

### Do you change my insulin?

No. Medication changes come from your doctor. A nurse monitors, teaches, and reports what she sees.

This page is general information, not medical advice. Always follow the instructions of your own doctor. Services are non-emergency. In a medical emergency, call 911.
