# High Blood Pressure Care at Home

High blood pressure often has no symptoms. Regular, accurate readings at home give your doctor better information.

## How a nurse can help

-   Check blood pressure the right way and compare it with your home cuff
-   Review medications and side effects
-   Talk through food, activity, and sleep habits your doctor recommends
-   Report trends to your doctor

## Questions, answered

### Is my home cuff accurate?

A nurse can compare your cuff with a reading taken by hand and show you how to take readings correctly.

This page is general information, not medical advice. Always follow the instructions of your own doctor. Services are non-emergency. In a medical emergency, call 911.
