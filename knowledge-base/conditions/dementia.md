# Dementia and Memory Care Support at Home

Dementia affects the whole family. A nurse can keep an eye on health, make medications safer, and help caregivers feel less alone.

## How a nurse can help

-   Set up a simple, safe medication system
-   Check for infections, dehydration, and pain that can look like confusion
-   Watch for changes that need the doctor
-   Teach family members what to expect and how to respond
-   Talk through when more help or a different setting may be needed

## Support for you

The Alzheimer's Association runs a free helpline, 1-800-272-3900, open 24 hours a day.

## Questions, answered

### Can a nurse stay all day?

Pierre Concierge Nursing provides scheduled visits. For daily hands on help, many families add a caregiver. We can help you work out the right mix.
