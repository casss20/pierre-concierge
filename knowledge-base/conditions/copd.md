# COPD Care at Home

COPD can flare up quickly. Knowing your normal and having a plan makes flare ups easier to catch.

## How a nurse can help

-   Check breathing, oxygen level, and vital signs
-   Review inhaler and nebulizer technique
-   Go over medications and how to take them
-   Help you set up a written action plan with your doctor

## Call your doctor if

-   Breathing is harder than usual
-   Your cough or phlegm changes
-   You need your rescue inhaler more often

If you think you or a loved one is having a medical emergency, call 911 right away. Do not wait for a nurse visit.

## Questions, answered

### Do you provide oxygen?

No. Oxygen is ordered by your doctor and supplied by a medical equipment company. A nurse can check that it is being used as prescribed.

This page is general information, not medical advice. Always follow the instructions of your own doctor. Services are non-emergency. In a medical emergency, call 911.
