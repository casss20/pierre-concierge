# Stroke Recovery at Home

Recovery continues long after the hospital stay. A nurse helps keep the plan on track and families informed.

## How a nurse can help

-   Check vital signs and watch for changes
-   Keep blood pressure and blood thinner medications on track as ordered
-   Look at swallowing, skin, and fall risks
-   Coordinate with therapists and your doctors

## Know the signs of a stroke

Sudden face drooping, arm weakness, speech trouble, vision loss, or loss of balance need emergency care.

If you think you or a loved one is having a medical emergency, call 911 right away. Do not wait for a nurse visit.

## Questions, answered

### Do you provide therapy?

No. Physical, occupational, and speech therapy come from licensed therapists. A nurse supports the overall plan.

This page is general information, not medical advice. Always follow the instructions of your own doctor. Services are non-emergency. In a medical emergency, call 911.
