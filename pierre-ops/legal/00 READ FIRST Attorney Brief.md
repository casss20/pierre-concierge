## Design rule
The contracting party in every client document is the BUSINESS (the Practice), not the nurse personally. The nurse signs as authorized representative and records her license number as the nurse giving care. This lets other nurses be added later.

## What is in this folder
- 01 Services: one folder per service (10 folders). Each holds what that service needs: 1 Signing Packet (the agreement plus that service's addendum, ready to sign), 2 Service Addendum, 3 Service Agreement, 4 HIPAA Notice of Privacy Practices, and 5 Client Intake Packet (every service except the free phone consultation). Each agreement and addendum has a formal client statement ("I, ____, have read..."), a statement for the Practice ("I, ____, a Florida licensed Registered Nurse, license number ____, sign on behalf of Pierre Concierge, LLC..."), and signature blocks for both.
- 02 Business Wide and Older Documents: the HIPAA notice, intake packet, referral form, and the older Independent Practice Service Agreement. That older agreement names the nurse personally and is SUPERSEDED by the Service Agreement in each service folder.
- 03 Website Policies and Disclaimer: text of the website Policies page and the privacy notice source.
- 04 Digital Product Terms: the "all sales final" and "not medical advice" terms for the PDF guides.
