Notice of Privacy Practices

Pierre Concierge Nursing & Wellness · Elda Marie Joe Pierre, RN, BSN
Effective date: ______________ · This notice applies to all client records

THIS NOTICE DESCRIBES HOW YOUR HEALTH INFORMATION MAY BE USED AND DISCLOSED, AND HOW YOU CAN GET ACCESS TO IT. PLEASE REVIEW IT CAREFULLY.

1. Who we are

Pierre Concierge Nursing ("the Practice") provides private-duty nursing services to clients in their homes, hotels, and residential communities. The Practice maintains the privacy of your protected health information (PHI) and follows the HIPAA Privacy Rule and professional nursing standards for all client records, whether or not a specific transaction requires it.

2. Uses and disclosures we may make WITHOUT your written authorization

  • For treatment — to coordinate your care with physicians, pharmacies, hospitals, labs, home health agencies, or other providers involved in your care.
  • For payment — to bill you for services, process payments, verify coverage, or provide a superbill you submit to an insurer.
  • For health care operations — for quality review, care planning, and as required for the management of the Practice.
  • To persons involved in your care — to family members, friends, or others you identify (or who are involved in your care or payment for care) as permitted by law, limited to information directly relevant to their involvement.
  • For emergencies — to prevent a serious and imminent threat to health or safety.
  • As required by law — including public health authorities, abuse or neglect reporting, health oversight agencies, court orders, law enforcement, and coroner/medical examiner as applicable.
  • Workers' compensation — as authorized by law, for work-related injury claims.

Other uses and disclosures (for example, marketing communications or the sale of information) will be made only with your written authorization, which you may revoke at any time in writing.

3. Your rights regarding your health information

  • Right to access and copy — inspect or obtain a copy of your health records, with limited exceptions, usually within 30 days of a written request.
  • Right to request an amendment — ask us to correct information you believe is inaccurate or incomplete.
  • Right to an accounting — receive a list of certain disclosures of your information.
  • Right to request restrictions — ask us to limit how we use or share your information. We will consider all requests (we are not required to agree to all restrictions).
  • Right to confidential communications — ask us to contact you at a specific address or by a specific method.
  • Right to a paper copy of this notice — at any time.

4. How to exercise your rights

Submit written requests to: Elda Marie Joe Pierre, RN, BSN — (754) 367-6929 · jpjojo565@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days where required. We may charge a reasonable, cost-based fee for copies as permitted by law.

5. Right to complain

If you believe your privacy rights have been violated, you may complain to the Practice directly at the contact above. You may also file a complaint with the U.S. Department of Health and Human Services, Office for Civil Rights: hhs.gov/ocr/privacy/hipaa/complaint/ · (800) 368-1019. We will never retaliate against you for filing a complaint.

6. Our duties

We are required by law to maintain the privacy and security of your protected health information, to provide you with this Notice of our legal duties and privacy practices, and to follow the terms of this Notice as long as it is in effect. We reserve the right to revise this Notice; the current version will be available on request and posted with new client paperwork.

7. How we protect your information

  • Records are stored electronically on the RN's secured, passcode-protected device — not in cloud services.
  • Physical documents are kept in a locked location.
  • Information is shared only with the people you designate, and only as needed for your care or payment.
  • Backups are encrypted and stored separately from the device.

8. Contact

Questions about this Notice or your information: Elda Marie Joe Pierre, RN, BSN · (754) 367-6929 · jpjojo565@gmail.com
