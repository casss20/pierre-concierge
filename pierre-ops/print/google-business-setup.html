Google Business Profile — Setup Guide

Pierre Concierge Nursing · do this once, in one sitting (~45 min) · everything here is written to copy-paste

Why this matters most

When a family in Orlando searches "private nurse Ocala" or "post-surgery care near me", Google shows the local pack — the map with 3 businesses — before any website. That pack is fed by Business Profiles, not websites. A well-run Profile with 20 reviews will outperform a beautiful website with zero reviews. This is the single highest-leverage free marketing asset she owns.

Before you start — one sharp decision: her current number, (754) 367-6929, is a South Florida area code. Locals notice; it quietly reads "not from here." Get a free Google Voice number with a 352 area code (voice.google.com) and use that number for the Business Profile, business cards, and the referral one-pager. Forward it to her cell — nothing changes operationally, but every Ocala local sees a hometown number. Keep the 754 as secondary on the Profile.

Part 1 — Create the profile

  1. Go to business.google.com and sign in with the Google account she wants to own it (recommend her main Gmail, not Anthony's).
  2. "Add your business." Enter the name exactly as: Pierre Concierge Nursing. Do NOT add keywords like "RN Home Care Ocala" — Google suspends profiles for that, and a suspension is painful to reverse.
  3. Category. Primary: Home health care service. Secondary: Nurse. (Type them; pick from the dropdown.)
  4. Location? Choose "Yes" to "Do you want to add a location customers can visit?" → then immediately mark it clear that she travels to clients: select "I deliver goods and services to my customers" and do not display the home address (she shouldn't publish her home). This makes it a service-area business.
  5. Service areas (type each, max ~20): Ocala · Silver Springs Shores · Belleview · The Villages · Dunnellon · Marion County.
  6. Contact. Phone: the 352 Google Voice number. Website: pierreconciergenursing.com.
  7. Verification. Google will offer video / phone / postcard. Video verification is fastest — she records herself outside (street + business signage or her car with branded clipboard), then inside showing equipment/supplies. Have the website open on a laptop in the video. Usually approved in 2–5 days.

Part 3 — The review engine (this is the whole ballgame)

  1. Get the review link. Profile → "Ask for reviews" → copy the short link (looks like g.page/r/…).
  2. Put it in the app. Practice Manager → Settings → "Google review link" → paste → Save. Now every completed visit has a Copy review ask button that writes the text message for her — she pastes it to the family contact.
  3. The moment to ask: right after a family receives the same-day report and says thank you. That's the gratitude peak — conversion is 5–10× higher than asking later. One ask per family; never pressure.
  4. QR card (optional but strong): print a small card — "How did we do? Scan to leave an honest review" — with the QR of the review link. Leave it on the counter at the last visit.
Never offer money, discounts, or gifts for reviews. It's against Google's policies and FTC rules, and a review purge after months of work is a real setback. Ask everyone — happy and merely satisfied — so the reviews stay honest and can't be flagged as incentivized.

Part 4 — Keep it alive (30 min/month)

  • Weekly post (Profile → Updates → Add update): one photo + 2–3 sentences. Rotation: a care tip ("The #1 reason seniors bounce back to the hospital is dehydration — here's what to watch for") · a credential/trust post · a seasonal note (hurricane-season med prep, heat + heart meds in July).
  • Answer every question and review within 24h — including negative ones, calmly and factually. Response rate is a ranking signal.
  • Monthly: add one fresh photo. Profiles with new photos rank better.

Part 5 — Consistency (one-time, 20 min)

Google cross-checks her name/phone/website across the web. Make these identical everywhere: business name, the 352 number, website. If you create a Facebook page or Yelp listing, use the exact same details. Mismatches dilute local ranking.

Timeline expectations: profile live in days, first reviews in weeks, visible local-pack movement typically after ~10–15 reviews and 2–3 months of activity. It compounds — reviews beget calls, calls beget clients, clients beget reviews.
