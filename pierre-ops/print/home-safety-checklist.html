AreaWhat to check✓Fixed / notes
LightingBright bulbs in halls, stairs & bathrooms; night light on path to bathroom; lamp within reach of bed☐
BathroomGrab bars at toilet & shower (not towel bars), non-slip mats, raised toilet seat, handheld shower head if needed☐
FloorsThrow rugs removed or taped down, cords out of walkways, clutter cleared, no polished/waxed floors☐
StairsHandrails both sides (or at least one), objects off steps, high-contrast tape on edges, good lighting at top & bottom☐
BedroomPhone & flashlight within reach, clear path to bathroom, bed height easy to stand from, nonslip rug beside bed☐
KitchenFrequently used items at waist height, no reaching over stove, working smoke detector & CO detector, fire extinguisher☐
MedicationsOrganized & labeled, expired items discarded, current list on fridge, no duplicate OTCs, reading glasses available☐
Mobility aidsWalker/cane fitted to height, rubber tips intact, wheelchair brakes work, no trailing slippers/loose laces☐
ExitsDoorways clear, spare key arrangement with family/neighbor, house numbers visible for EMS, deadbolts easy to open☐
Emergency911 posted by phone, emergency contacts list on fridge, charged phone, medical info (conditions/allergies) visible☐
Hydration & foodEasy-access water, non-perishable meals available, pet food if applicable — dehydration is the #1 reason seniors bounce back to the hospital☐
