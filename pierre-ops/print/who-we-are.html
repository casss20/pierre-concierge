Most families find us at one of two moments — the week of a hospital discharge, or the day caring for Mom or Dad starts feeling bigger than the family.

Meet your nurse

Elda Marie Joe Pierre, RN, BSN, is a Registered Nurse with over two decades of hands-on experience — from the fast pace of rehabilitation centers to the personal setting of home health care.

She founded Pierre Concierge Nursing after seeing too many patients sent home with confusing instructions and little support. Her answer is simple: one experienced nurse, unhurried visits, and a family that always knows what's going on — because the person providing the care is the person answering the phone.

Every visit is delivered by Elda herself.

The gap we fill

  • Discharge moves fast. Instructions pile up, medications change, and the first night home arrives before anyone feels ready.
  • Coverage is thin. Medicare home health visits, when they come, are short and task-focused — and they pause the moment a patient isn't "homebound."
  • Families are stretched. Adult children often live hours away and get their updates from a five-minute phone call.

We work right alongside your physicians and any home health team — and fill the space in between.

What families get

  • The same nurse, every visit — someone who knows you, your history, and what matters to your family
  • Unhurried, RN-level care at home — typically an hour or more, not a rushed task list
  • A written same-day report to family — vitals, medications, observations, and honest answers, by evening
  • One number that reaches your nurse directly — no call centers, no waiting on hold
  • Honest, posted pricing — no insurance games, no surprise bills
