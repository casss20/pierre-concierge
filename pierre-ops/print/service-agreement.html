1. Services

The Practice provides non-emergency private-duty nursing services selected by the client from the current fee schedule, including (as applicable): post-operative recovery support, hospital-to-home transition, advanced wound care, IV therapy & hydration, medication management & reconciliation, assisted-living support, family peace-of-mind visits, wellness assessments, chronic care plan visits, and patient advocacy. Specific services, frequency, and duration are agreed in writing (care plan) and may change with mutual consent.

2. What this practice is — and is not

  1. The Practice is an independent RN-owned private-pay nursing service. It is not a Medicare/Medicaid home health agency and does not bill insurance. A superbill may be provided for the client's own reimbursement attempts.
  2. Services are provided within the scope of Registered Nurse practice under the Florida Nurse Practice Act. The Practice does not diagnose, prescribe, or alter physician orders; all care is coordinated with, and remains under the authority of, the client's own physician(s).
  3. The Practice does not provide emergency or 911 services, live-in care, or 24/7 on-call coverage unless expressly included in a written package. In an emergency the client or family must call 911 first.

3. Client responsibilities

  1. Provide accurate and complete health information, medication lists, and physician contacts.
  2. Maintain an appropriate physician relationship for ongoing medical care.
  3. Provide a reasonably safe home environment (our home safety checklist is available).
  4. Follow the agreed care plan and take medications as prescribed by their physician.
  5. Inform the Practice of hospitalizations, new diagnoses, or changes in condition between visits.

4. Fees & payment

  1. Fees are charged as listed on the current fee schedule (attached or as quoted in writing). Packages are payable in advance; per-visit services are payable at the time of service unless otherwise agreed in writing.
  2. Invices are payable within 14 days. Payment methods:
  3. A late fee of $25 may be applied to balances over 30 days. Visits may be paused for accounts over 45 days past due, after written notice.
  4. Supplies and travel outside Marion County may incur additional charges quoted in advance.

5. Scheduling, cancellation & missed visits

  1. Visits are scheduled in advance by phone or text. The Practice confirms each scheduled visit.
  2. Client cancellation: please give at least 24 hours' notice. Same-day or no-show visits may be billed at 50% of the scheduled fee.
  3. Practice cancellation: if the Practice must cancel (illness, weather, emergency), the client is notified as soon as possible and the visit is rescheduled at no charge.
  4. The separate Missed-Visit & Cancellation Policy attached to this agreement applies in full.

6. Records & privacy

Client health information is handled as described in the Notice of Privacy Practices (included in this packet). Records are maintained on a secured, passcode-protected device in the Practice's control. The client may request copies of their records at any time.

7. No guarantee of outcomes

Services are provided professionally and in good faith, in accordance with nursing standards. However, individual health outcomes depend on many factors outside the Practice's control (including the client's underlying conditions and adherence to their physician's and care team's instructions). The Practice does not guarantee any particular medical outcome.

8. Termination

Either party may end this agreement at any time, with or without cause, by giving notice (verbal or written). Fees for services already provided remain due. Upon termination, records are retained per professional standards and remain available to the client.

9. General

  1. This agreement is governed by Florida law; venue is Marion County, Florida.
  2. If any provision is found unenforceable, the remainder stays in effect.
  3. This is the entire agreement between the parties and replaces prior verbal understandings. Changes must be in writing (text or email is acceptable).

Missed-Visit & Cancellation Policy

Client-facing summary — keep with your intake packet

Scheduling

Visits are scheduled in advance and confirmed by text or phone. Standing weekly visits can be reserved on the same day/time each week.

If you need to cancel

  • More than 24 hours' notice: no charge — we'll reschedule.
  • Less than 24 hours / same day: 50% of the scheduled visit fee.
  • No-show (not home, unable to enter, or unavailable at the scheduled time): 50% of the scheduled visit fee.
  • Cancellation fees are waived for genuine emergencies (hospitalization, 911 event) — just let us know when you can.

If we need to cancel

Rarely, illness, severe weather, or an emergency may require us to cancel. You'll be notified as soon as possible, and the visit will be rescheduled at no charge. Scheduled package visits affected by Practice cancellation are credited or made up.

Late arrivals & waiting

We allow a 15-minute grace window for client availability at the scheduled start time, then the visit may count as a no-show. If the Practice runs late, your full scheduled visit time is honored or pro-rated fairly.

Inclement weather

During hurricane warnings or hazardous conditions, visits may be rescheduled for safety. Telephonic check-ins may substitute when appropriate.
