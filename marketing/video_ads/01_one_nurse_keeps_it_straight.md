## Direction card (directed with the pierre ad director skill)

| | |
|---|---|
| Idea in one sentence | After a hospital stay, one nurse turns a pile of papers into one clear page. |
| Benchmark | Quiet documentary scenes of home life: a low, still camera, long holds, one slow push. Learn the stillness and the held composition. Do not take the sadness or the talking head interview. |
| Structures rejected | A fast montage listing every service. A nurse introducing herself to the camera. |
| Look | Soft morning window light, warm grade, pine green in the shadows, ivory and sand highlights. |
| Accent and its one meaning | Brass is the warm light that falls on the one printed page. Nothing else in the frame is gold. Meaning: the answer. |
| The constant | The kitchen table, same position and same light in shots 1, 2, 4, 5 and the poster frame. |
| The one moment | 15 to 20 s, shot 4. Anticipation: the nurse rests her hand flat on the page for a beat. Release: she slides it across to the older man. Settle: the page stops square in front of him, nobody moves, hold. |
| The hook | 'Who keeps track?' on screen at 0.0 s, held to 3 s, over the messy table. Anticipation: the tired hands freeze over the pile. Release: they sweep it into two piles. Settle: one hand rests flat. |
| Eye path | Papers (1), her face (2), the door (3), the page (4), the daughter breathing out (5), the tidy table (6). |
| Empty space for text | Top third calm in every shot. In shot 1 the papers stay in the lower two thirds. In shot 4 the page sits center left and the right third stays empty. |
| The ending | The shot 1 table, now tidy, one page centered with warm light on it. It answers the opening mess. |
| Avoid in this ad | The nurse smiling at the camera. Pill bottles with labels. Any readable text on the papers. |

Style switches: photoreal look, so smear frames and animating on twos are OFF. Camera is a slow push in, a held frame, or a gentle drift. No whip pans, no handheld shake.

## Edit plan and timing

| Moment | When | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| Hook text on screen | 0.0 to 3 s | Large, high contrast, readable with the sound off by 3 seconds. Frame 0 is already a finished composition. |
| The one moment (hero) | 15 to 20 s | Build toward it. Anticipation, release, settle each visible on a phone. Do not spend it earlier. |
| The breath | 20 to 21.5 s, the daughter reads the page in silence. | Delay the shot 5 voiceover by 1.5 seconds so the silence is real. Room tone only. |
| Pace | whole ad | Shots 1 and 2 are quickest, with the action starting on frame 0. Shot 4 holds longest. Shot 5 is the breath and ends on a breath out, not a smile. |
| End card poster | 25 to 30 s | Generated poster frame held 2 s, then a flat color end card with logo lockup, one line, website and Serving Ocala and Marion County. |

Generation tip: each shot prompt holds one action only. If the generator adds a second action, trim the clip at the end of the first action and re roll with a simpler prompt.
