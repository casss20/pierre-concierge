## Direction card (directed with the pierre ad director skill)

| | |
|---|---|
| Idea in one sentence | A question at night gets an answer from a person, not a phone tree. |
| Benchmark | Night scenes lit by one lamp and a phone. Learn the single source light and the quiet room tone. Do not take thriller tension. |
| Structures rejected | A long chat transcript on screen. A split screen of hold music against text messages. |
| Look | Every shot is evening, lit by one lamp and the phone. The nurse cutaway is soft evening light too. The relief comes from the warm glow, not from daylight. |
| Accent and its one meaning | Brass is the warm glow of the reply. Meaning: a person answered. |
| The constant | The phone in the man's hands across shots 1, 2, 4 and 5. |
| The one moment | 15 to 20 s, shot 4 only. Anticipation: his thumb hovers above the phone and the room holds quiet. Release: the screen lights his face with a warm glow as the reply arrives, one soft chime. Settle: he reads, breathes out, holds. |
| The hook | 'A question. Not an emergency.' on screen at 0.0 s, held to 3 s. The ad opens on the glowing phone and the camera pulls back to reveal him. Anticipation: he glances at the bedroom door. Release: the camera finishes its pull back. Settle: he holds still. |
| Eye path | The glowing phone (1), the phone menu (2), the nurse typing (3), the warm screen on his face (4), the phone face down (5), the poster (6). |
| Empty space for text | Dark empty space above his head for the hook. Lamp at the left edge, never at the top. The 911 line on the end card sits above the bottom fifth. |
| The ending | The phone face down on the arm of the sofa, lamp glow warm, the man settled. It answers the opening, where the phone was the center of his worry. |
| Avoid in this ad | Any readable message or health detail in a chat bubble. A cold blue grade. Close ups of fingers typing with distorted hands. |

Style switches: photoreal look, so smear frames and animating on twos are OFF. Camera is a slow push in, a held frame, or a gentle drift. No whip pans, no handheld shake.

## Edit plan and timing

| Moment | When | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| Hook text on screen | 0.0 to 3 s | Large, high contrast, readable with the sound off by 3 seconds. Frame 0 is already a finished composition. |
| The one moment (hero) | 15 to 20 s | Build toward it. Anticipation, release, settle each visible on a phone. Do not spend it earlier. |
| The breath | 20 to 21.5 s, he sets the phone down and the room is quiet. | Delay the shot 5 voiceover by 1.5 seconds so the silence is real. Room tone only. |
| Pace | whole ad | Shots 1 and 2 are slow and dim. The cut to the nurse is the acceleration. Shot 4 is the only moment with a warm glow on his face. Shot 5 holds on room tone. |
| End card poster | 25 to 30 s | Generated poster frame held 2 s, then a flat color end card with logo lockup, one line, website, phone and Serving Ocala and Marion County. |

Generation tip: each shot prompt holds one action only. If the generator adds a second action, trim the clip at the end of the first action and re roll with a simpler prompt.
