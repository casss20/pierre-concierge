## Direction card

| | |
|---|---|
| Idea in one sentence | One checklist tucked into a hospital bag makes the trip home after surgery calm. |
| Benchmark | Gentle "how we did it" vlogs where hands do small careful tasks in order. Learn the close, tactile hands and the step by step rhythm. Do not take the talking to camera or the jump cuts. |
| Structures rejected | A full screen scroll through the PDF. A nurse explaining the checklist to camera. |
| Look | Soft daylight throughout, ivory walls, sand linens, pine green shadows. |
| Accent and its one meaning | Brass is the warm light on the checklist page. Shots 2 and 6 only. Meaning: the plan. |
| The constant | The printed checklist, which travels from the bag (2) to the hospital (3) to the side table (5, 6). |
| The one moment | 21 s, shot 5. Anticipation: his pen hovers over the last box. Release: one tick. Settle: the pen lifts away, hold. |
| Motion beats | Shot 2: page laid in, hold. Shot 4: rug carried out, hold on the clear hallway. Shot 5: hover, tick, pen lifts, hold. |
| Eye path | Folded cardigan (1), the page on top of the clothes (2), his pen (3), the clear hallway (4), the last tick (5), the page beside the chair (6). |
| Empty space for text | Top third calm in every shot (plain wall or window). Bottom fifth calm. |
| The ending | The checklist on the side table next to her chair in warm light. It answers the packed bag. |
| Avoid in this ad | Surgical wounds, dressings, IV lines, machines, hospital branding. Readable checklist text (use the real PDF only on the end card). Any question to the viewer about their own surgery. |

Style switches: photoreal, smear frames and animating on twos OFF. Camera: held frame or slow push in.
