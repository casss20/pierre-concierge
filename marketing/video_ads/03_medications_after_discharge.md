## Caption for the post

Going home is not the end of the hard part. If your family is managing new medications after a hospital stay, a Pierre Concierge registered nurse can review them with you at home and leave a clear written summary. Serving Ocala and Marion County. Book a free phone consultation.

## Check before publishing

- Do not claim this prevents readmissions, errors or complications. The ad says a nurse reviews medications and writes a summary, nothing more.
- No readable drug names, doses or labels in any generated frame.
- Do not name a condition. Keep the situation generic.
