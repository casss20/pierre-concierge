## Direction card (directed with the pierre ad director skill)

| | |
|---|---|
| Idea in one sentence | A bag of new medications becomes one calm page the whole family can read. |
| Benchmark | Gentle homecoming scenes in warm afternoon light. Learn the unhurried pacing and the closeness to hands and objects. Do not take melodrama or hospital drama. |
| Structures rejected | A look inside the hospital and the discharge desk. A close up of pills as the lead image. |
| Look | Warm afternoon light, honey tones, pine green in the shadows, ivory highlights. |
| Accent and its one meaning | Brass is the warm light on the one written summary page. Meaning: clarity. |
| The constant | The same kitchen counter and table angle in shots 2, 3, 4 and 5. |
| The one moment | 20 to 25 s, shot 5. Anticipation: the daughter holds the page flat against the refrigerator door. Release: she presses a magnet over it. Settle: she steps back and holds still, her mother smiling behind her. |
| The hook | 'Home is only the start' on screen at 0.0 s, held to 3 s, over a car at a curb with open sky above. Anticipation: the daughter pauses at the open door. Release: she helps her mother out. Settle: they stand together for a beat. |
| Eye path | The car door (1), the bottles in her hands (2), the nurse at the counter (3), the pill organizer and page (4), the page on the fridge (5), the poster (6). |
| Empty space for text | Open sky above the car in shot 1. In shot 4 the table top is the subject and the upper third stays clear. |
| The ending | The refrigerator door with one page held by a magnet. It answers the opening, where the paper bag was a burden. |
| Avoid in this ad | A readable label, dose or drug name. Any hospital building, logo, wheelchair or sliding doors. A pill spilling in slow motion. |

Style switches: photoreal look, so smear frames and animating on twos are OFF. Camera is a slow push in, a held frame, or a gentle drift. No whip pans, no handheld shake.

## Edit plan and timing

| Moment | When | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| Hook text on screen | 0.0 to 3 s | Large, high contrast, readable with the sound off by 3 seconds. Frame 0 is already a finished composition. |
| The one moment (hero) | 20 to 25 s | Build toward it. Anticipation, release, settle each visible on a phone. Do not spend it earlier. |
| The breath | 20 to 21.5 s, the page on the refrigerator and quiet. | Delay the shot 5 voiceover by 1.5 seconds so the silence is real. Room tone only. |
| Pace | whole ad | Shot 1 is calm but moving. Shot 2 slows into worry. The nurse already being at the counter is the turn. Shot 5 holds longest. |
| End card poster | 25 to 30 s | Generated poster frame held 2 s, then a flat color end card with logo lockup, one line, website and Serving Ocala and Marion County. |

Generation tip: each shot prompt holds one action only. If the generator adds a second action, trim the clip at the end of the first action and re roll with a simpler prompt.
