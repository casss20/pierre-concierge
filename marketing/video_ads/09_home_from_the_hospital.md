## Direction card

| | |
|---|---|
| Idea in one sentence | A discharge folder that overwhelms a son stops being his alone once a nurse goes through it with him. |
| Benchmark | Calm product films where one object carries the story from chaos to order. Learn the object as the hero and the clean top down framing. Do not take the fast product cuts or the upbeat music. |
| Structures rejected | A hospital scene with machines and staff. A before and after split screen. |
| Look | Late afternoon kitchen light, warm grade, pine green in the shadows, ivory counter, sand colored folder. |
| Accent and its one meaning | Brass is the warm light on the folder once it has been explained. Shots 4 and 6 only. Meaning: explained, not just handed over. |
| The constant | The sand colored folder on the kitchen counter, same spot in shots 1, 2, 4 and 6. |
| The one moment | 17 s, shot 4. Anticipation: nurse and son stand over the open folder. Release: she turns the folder to face him. Settle: her hand rests flat beside it, he leans in, hold. |
| Motion beats | Shot 1: folder lands, stillness. Shot 4: turn the folder, hand rests, hold. Shot 5: the son leans back, hold. |
| Eye path | The folder landing (1), the son's hand on his forehead (2), father in his chair (3), the folder turning toward him (4), father and son together (5), the closed folder with a mug beside it (6). |
| Empty space for text | Counter shots framed with the folder in the lower half and plain wall above. Bottom fifth calm in every shot. |
| The ending | The shot 1 counter, the folder closed and squared, a coffee mug beside it, warm light. It answers the papers sliding out. |
| Avoid in this ad | Hospital rooms, beds, wristbands, machines. Pill bottles and labels. Readable papers. A cane or wheelchair as a prop for pity. Smiling at the camera. |

Style switches: photoreal, smear frames and animating on twos OFF. Camera: slow push in, held frame, gentle drift.

## Full voiceover (48 words)

The hospital sent him home with a folder.

Page after page. And a lot of questions.

A registered nurse from Pierre Concierge Nursing comes to the house, here in Ocala. She goes through it with them, page by page.

So nobody reads it alone.

Book a free call.

## Caption

Hospitals send families home with a folder. Pierre Concierge Nursing sends a registered nurse to homes in Ocala and Marion County to go through it with the family: the medications, the follow up visits and what to watch for. Book a free phone consultation. Non emergency services. In an emergency, call 911.
