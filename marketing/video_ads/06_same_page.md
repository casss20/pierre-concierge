## Direction card (directed with the pierre ad director skill)

| | |
|---|---|
| Idea in one sentence | Three siblings who disagree read the same written page and start from the same facts. |
| Benchmark | Ensemble family scenes with several faces in one frame and no raised voices. Learn how to read tension from posture and distance. Do not take shouting or slammed doors. |
| Structures rejected | A courtroom style split between two sides. A mediator figure making a speech. |
| Look | Dining room, neutral daylight, slightly desaturated at the start, warming as they read the page. |
| Accent and its one meaning | Brass is the one white page, warming to gold as it is shared. Meaning: shared facts. |
| The constant | The dining table, in shots 1, 2, 4 and 5. |
| The one moment | 20 to 25 s, shot 5. Anticipation: all three look down at the same page. Release: their faces soften together. Settle: one nods slowly and the others follow, hold. |
| The hook | 'Families do not always agree.' on screen by 0.5 s, held to 3 s. Anticipation: one sibling crosses her arms. Release: the others look away. Settle: all three hold still. |
| Eye path | The three faces (1), the three phones (2), the nurse writing (3), the laptop on the table (4), the page and the faces (5), the hands on one page (6). |
| Empty space for text | Table surface in the lower third stays clear. The mother stays soft and blurred in the background, never in the text zone. |
| The ending | Three hands resting on one single page on the dining table. It answers the opening of crossed arms. |
| Avoid in this ad | Shouting, finger pointing or a slammed table. Any readable text on the page. Taking sides between siblings. |

Style switches: photoreal look, so smear frames and animating on twos are OFF. Camera is a slow push in, a held frame, or a gentle drift. No whip pans, no handheld shake.

## Full voiceover

When a parent needs more help, families do not always agree. Everyone has an opinion. Nobody has the same information.

A Pierre Concierge nurse visits, and writes it down in plain language. One written report. One family call. Everyone hears the same thing. It will not settle every debate. Same facts, though.

Get everyone on the same page. Book a free call.

## Edit plan and timing

| Moment | When | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| Hook text on screen | 0 to 3 s | Large, high contrast, readable with the sound off by 3 seconds. Frame 0 is already a finished composition. |
| The one moment (hero) | 20 to 25 s | Build toward it. Anticipation, release, settle each visible on a phone. Do not spend it earlier. |
| The breath | 20 to 21.5 s, three people reading in silence. | Delay the shot 5 voiceover by 1.5 seconds so the silence is real. Room tone only. |
| Pace | whole ad | Shots 1 and 2 cut quickly with tension. The nurse's shot is the turn. Shot 5 holds longest. |
| End card poster | 25 to 30 s | Generated poster frame held 2 s, then a flat color end card with logo lockup, one line, website, phone and Serving Ocala and Marion County. |

Generation tip: each shot prompt holds one action only. If the generator adds a second action, trim the clip at the end of the first action and re roll with a simpler prompt.
