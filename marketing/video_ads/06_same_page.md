## Sound

Start with a low, slightly tense hum, then switch to warm acoustic at the 10 second mark when the nurse appears.

## Alternate hooks for testing

- "When the family cannot agree on care, start with the same information."
- "The hardest part of caring for a parent is sometimes your siblings."

## Caption for the post

When a parent needs more help, families do not always agree. A written report and a monthly family call from a Pierre Concierge nurse gives everyone the same facts. Serving Ocala and Marion County. Book a free phone consultation.

## Check before publishing

- Keep the honest line "It will not settle every debate." It protects the ad from over promising.
- Never imply the nurse takes sides or decides for the family.
- No real family situations or named conditions.
