# Ad 8: The Sunday Night Question

| | |
|---|---|
| Problem | An adult child cannot tell whether anyone is keeping track of a parent's care |
| Audience | Daughters and sons, 40 to 60, with a parent living at home in Ocala or Marion County |
| Plan featured | Nurse visits with medication review, appointment notes and a written summary (no plan name, no price) |
| Length and format | 30 seconds, vertical 9:16, six shots of 5 seconds |
| Call to action | Book a free phone consultation |
| Status | Directed v3: hook tests, muted read and a 15 second cut added (v2 was fixed after independent review). Needs Elda's review. |

## Direction card

| | |
|---|---|
| Idea in one sentence | A daughter who checks her phone all Sunday night can finally put it down once a nurse is keeping track. |
| Benchmark | Quiet slice of life scenes in home insurance and family phone ads: one lamp, one face, long holds. Learn the stillness and the lit phone as the emotional center. Do not take the sad piano swell or the voice that speaks to the viewer as "you". |
| Structures rejected | A list of services over stock footage. The daughter talking to camera about her mother. |
| Look | Night: one warm table lamp, cool phone glow, deep pine green shadows. Day: soft morning window light, ivory and sand highlights. |
| Accent and its one meaning | Brass is the warm light on her hand resting on the face down phone. Shots 5 and 6 only. Meaning: she can let go. |
| The constant | The daughter's phone: lit in her hand at night (shot 1), face down at rest (shots 5 and 6). |
| The one moment | 21 s, shot 5. Anticipation: her hand hovers over the phone on the desk. Release: she turns it face down. Settle: her hand stays flat on it, she leans back a little, hold. |
| Motion beats | Shot 1: phone lowers to her lap, hold. Shot 4: pen laid down, hold. Shot 5: hover, phone face down, hand rests, hold. |
| Eye path | Her face in phone glow (1), the falling sticky note (2), the opening door (3), the pen on the table (4), her hand on the face down phone (5), the empty sofa with the phone at rest (6). |
| Empty space for text | Top third dark and calm in shot 1. Fridge framed in the lower two thirds in shot 2. Bottom fifth calm in every shot for platform buttons. |
| The ending | The same sofa as shot 1, now in morning light, lamp off, phone face down on the cushion. It answers the lit phone at night. |
| Avoid in this ad | Tears. The daughter smiling at the camera. Readable text on the phone, fridge notes or page. Pill bottles. Any "you" sentence about the viewer's family or health. |

Style switches: photoreal, so smear frames and animating on twos are OFF. Camera: slow push in, held frame, or gentle drift only.

## Full voiceover (49 words)

Sunday night. She wonders who is keeping track of Mom's care.

Appointments. New medications. Questions nobody wrote down.

A registered nurse from Pierre Concierge Nursing visits her mother at home in Ocala. She reviews the medications and writes it all down.

Now someone is keeping track.

Book a free call.

Voice: one calm female voice, third person throughout. The voice describes the daughter on screen, never the viewer.
