## Direction card

| | |
|---|---|
| Idea in one sentence | A daughter in another state goes from a missed call to a plan on her desk. |
| Benchmark | Calm product films where one object carries the story from mess to order. Learn the object as the hero and the held close ups. Do not take fast product cuts, price shouting or countdown timers. |
| Structures rejected | A page by page scroll through the PDF. A nurse reading the guide to camera. |
| Look | Soft daylight or warm lamp light, ivory and sand highlights, pine green shadows. |
| Accent and its one meaning | Brass is the warm light on the printed care team sheet. Shots 3 and 4 only. Meaning: in control. |
| The constant | Her office desk, in shots 1, 2, 4 and 5. |
| The one moment | 17 s, shot 4. Anticipation: she holds the phone ready with a printed call script beside her laptop. Release: someone answers and she nods once. Settle: she sits back, hold. |
| Eye path | The hands in shot 1, the face in shot 2, the guide in shot 3, the moment in shot 4, the calm in shot 5, the real cover at the end. |
| Empty space for text | Text zone: between 15 and 40 percent of the frame height (about y 200 to 500). Nothing in the bottom 25 percent. |
| The ending | The real cover on ivory, which answers the mess in shot 1. |
| Avoid in this ad | Medical settings, readable phone screens or pages, a generated cover, legal advice. Any line that asks the viewer about their parent. |

Style switches: photoreal, smear frames and animating on twos OFF. Camera: slow push in, held frame or gentle drift.
