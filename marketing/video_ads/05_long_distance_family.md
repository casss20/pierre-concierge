Gentle strings or piano. Soft, a touch emotional, hopeful at the end.

## Alternate hooks for testing

- "Caring for a parent from far away is its own kind of hard."
- "You cannot be in two places. A nurse can be in one."

## Caption for the post

If your parent lives in Ocala or Marion County and you do not, the distance is hard. The Family Link Plan gives you a monthly video call with a Pierre Concierge nurse and a written report. Book a free phone consultation.

## Check before publishing

- The client receiving care must be located in Florida. State this in the landing page, not in a way that confuses the ad.
- Do not target by health condition. Target by interest and location (for example people with family in Marion County), following the platform's rules for health related advertising.
- Do not say the nurse replaces a care manager or a doctor.
- The Family Link wording ("appointment coordination", "monthly written report") must match what Elda actually delivers.
