## Direction card (directed with the pierre ad director skill)

| | |
|---|---|
| Idea in one sentence | A monthly video call and a written report close the distance between a daughter in the city and her parent in Ocala. |
| Benchmark | Two places, one connection: a city apartment at night and a sunny Florida living room. Learn the contrast of light between them. Do not take the sadness of separation films. |
| Structures rejected | A map with a line drawn between two cities. A montage of airport scenes. |
| Look | City shots are cooler and darker, blue hour. Florida shots are warm, bright and green. The poster frame is the city window in warm morning light. |
| Accent and its one meaning | Brass is the warm glow of the laptop screen in the Florida living room. Meaning: connection. |
| The constant | The laptop, in shots 2, 4 and 5. |
| The one moment | 15 to 20 s, shot 4. Anticipation: the nurse smooths a cushion beside the older mother. Release: the mother waves at the glowing laptop once. Settle: both hold still, smiling. |
| The hook | 'A parent in Ocala. A family far away.' on screen by 0.5 s, held to 3 s, over the city window. Anticipation: she looks at the phone. Release: she lowers it. Settle: she looks out at the lights. |
| Eye path | The window and skyline (1), the laptop with a blurred calendar (2), the nurse and the older woman laughing (3), the glowing laptop and the wave (4), the daughter reading the report (5), the morning window (6). |
| Empty space for text | The skyline gives open sky for the hook in shot 1. In shot 4 the laptop is centered with soft blurred space around it. |
| The ending | The same city window in morning light, the woman relaxed and the phone face down on the sill. It answers the opening, where she stood at night looking at the lights. |
| Avoid in this ad | A map or a plane. A fake video call with readable text. A face shown inside a laptop screen. The mother alone and sad. |

Style switches: photoreal look, so smear frames and animating on twos are OFF. Camera is a slow push in, a held frame, or a gentle drift. No whip pans, no handheld shake.

## Edit plan and timing

| Moment | When | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| Hook text on screen | 0 to 3 s | Large, high contrast, readable with the sound off by 3 seconds. Frame 0 is already a finished composition. |
| The one moment (hero) | 15 to 20 s | Build toward it. Anticipation, release, settle each visible on a phone. Do not spend it earlier. |
| The breath | 20 to 21.5 s, the daughter reads the report in quiet. | Delay the shot 5 voiceover by 1.5 seconds so the silence is real. Room tone only. |
| Pace | whole ad | The city shots are slower. The cut to Florida is the acceleration. Shot 4 holds longest. Shot 5 is the breath. |
| End card poster | 25 to 30 s | Generated poster frame held 2 s, then a flat color end card with logo lockup, one line, website, phone and Serving Ocala and Marion County. |

Generation tip: each shot prompt holds one action only. If the generator adds a second action, trim the clip at the end of the first action and re roll with a simpler prompt.
