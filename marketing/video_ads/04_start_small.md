## Direction card (directed with the pierre ad director skill)

| | |
|---|---|
| Idea in one sentence | One visit a month is a real way in for a family that thinks private nursing is too expensive. |
| Benchmark | Simple household scenes shot warm and hopeful, a kitchen table and a calendar. Learn the plain honest look. Do not take stress cliches. |
| Structures rejected | A price comparison chart. A talking head reassuring the viewer about cost. |
| Look | Warm evening kitchen, sand and ivory, pine green accents. Gentle, not gloomy. |
| Accent and its one meaning | Brass is the ink circle on the calendar. Meaning: the one visit. |
| The constant | The wall calendar, shown in shots 1, 4 and the poster frame, and the kitchen table in shots 1, 2 and 5. |
| The one moment | 15 to 20 s, shot 4. Anticipation: a hand holds a brass colored pen still above one date. Release: a single circle is drawn. Settle: the pen lifts and stops, the calendar holds. |
| The hook | 'Private nursing can feel out of reach.' on screen by 0.5 s, held to 3 s. The ad opens on a blank wall calendar and slowly pulls back to reveal a man at the table. Anticipation: he stops tapping the calculator. Release: the camera finishes its pull back. Settle: he holds still. |
| Eye path | The blank calendar (1), his mother in the window (2), the nurse and the page (3), the circle being drawn (4), the shared tea (5), the same calendar with one circled date (6). |
| Empty space for text | The wall around the calendar stays empty for text. In shot 4 the calendar is centered with empty wall around it. |
| The ending | The same wall calendar from shot 1, now with one circled date. It answers the opening: from blank to one clear plan. |
| Avoid in this ad | Red bills or piles of money. A price shown before Elda confirms it. A face of despair. |

Style switches: photoreal look, so smear frames and animating on twos are OFF. Camera is a slow push in, a held frame, or a gentle drift. No whip pans, no handheld shake.

## Edit plan and timing

| Moment | When | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| Hook text on screen | 0 to 3 s | Large, high contrast, readable with the sound off by 3 seconds. Frame 0 is already a finished composition. |
| The one moment (hero) | 15 to 20 s | Build toward it. Anticipation, release, settle each visible on a phone. Do not spend it earlier. |
| The breath | 20 to 21.5 s, tea at the table with a still room. | Delay the shot 5 voiceover by 1.5 seconds so the silence is real. Room tone only. |
| Pace | whole ad | Shot 1 is the quickest and most tense. The cut to shot 2 relaxes. Shot 4 is the quietest and holds longest. |
| End card poster | 25 to 30 s | Generated poster frame held 2 s, then a flat color end card with logo lockup, one line, website, phone and Serving Ocala and Marion County. |

Generation tip: each shot prompt holds one action only. If the generator adds a second action, trim the clip at the end of the first action and re roll with a simpler prompt.
